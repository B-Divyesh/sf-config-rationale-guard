Privacy, in plain text.

The CLI is offline and has no telemetry. The browser demo processes its inputs only in your browser.

What stays local

Configuration, rationale text, demo input, and generated reports are not sent to us. The site does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party scripts.

License storage

If you buy or restore the Team unlock, the license token and its latest verification result are stored in your browser’s local storage under keys beginning with sb_license:config-rationale-guard. You can remove them by clearing this site’s storage.

License verification and checkout

License verification sends only your license token to the Sociobot billing API. Purchases open a Sociobot/Dodo hosted checkout, where the merchant of record processes the contact and payment details needed for the transaction. Their policies apply to that checkout.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
